# Cinova Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 14, 2026**

## Data We Collect

- **Anonymous session ID**: A random UUID stored locally on your device. Used to maintain your session without requiring an account.
- **Email and username**: Only if you choose to create an account.
- **Interaction data**: Movies you save, rate, or dismiss — used to improve recommendations. Stored on our servers.
- **Country/region**: Used to show streaming availability for your location.
- **Push notification token**: Only if you grant permission.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not sell your data to third parties.
- We do not track your location beyond country-level.
- We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone.

## Third-Party Services

- **TMDB**: Movie metadata from The Movie Database (themoviedb.org). See their privacy policy.
- **Google AdMob**: Advertising (free tier). See Google's privacy policy.
